# Parking Building Management System (PBMS) — Developer Business Rules Registry (v2.0)

This registry documents the formal **Developer-Understanding-Type (Technical Specification)** business rules, programmatic constraints, and API mappings of the **Parking Building Management System (PBMS)**. 

All **70 business rules** are extracted directly from `PBMSystemBusinessRules_Professional.xlsx` in your Downloads directory. They are structured into tabular layouts to allow developers to copy and paste them directly into **Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets** to maintain the Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) and QA Test Cases.

---

These rules govern the programmatic database relationships, unique keys, schema validations, and soft-delete policies of the system.

| **BR-STR-09** | **Email Uniqueness** <br> `POST /auth/register` | Mỗi địa chỉ email chỉ được đăng ký MỘT tài khoản duy nhất.<br><br>*Each email address must be unique in the system.* | Triggered during user signup or profile email update. | Programmatic check: `_db.Users.AnyAsync(u => u.Email == email)`. | **BR-GEN-08** | **Notification Read Tracking** <br> `POST /auth/forgot-password` | Hệ thống theo dõi trạng thái đã đọc của tối đa 20 thông báo.<br><br>*Alert tracking handles read/unread flags.* | Triggered on `/notifications/read` or GET `/notifications`. | Updates unread states in DB. | **BR-GEN-10** | **Privacy Protection** <br> `POST /auth/register` | Quy trình quên mật khẩu không được tiết lộ sự tồn tại của email.<br><br>*Password reset requests prevent email harvesting.* | Triggered on `/auth/password/forgot` API call. | Always returns HTTP 200 Success regardless of whether the email exists in the database. | `200 OK`<br> `message: "Nếu email này hợp lệ, mã OTP đã được gửi."` | **Given** an unregistered email,<br>**When** forgot password is submitted,<br>**Then** return `200 OK`. |

| **BR-PKM-10** | **Entry Time Recording** <br> `GET /reservations/:id` | Ghi nhận thời gian vào thực tế làm căn cứ tính phí.<br><br>*Accurate UTC entry timestamping.* | Triggered when physical check-in completes. | Sets `EntryTime = DateTime.UtcNow` inside the session table. | `200 OK`<br> `data: { entryTime }` | **Given** a gate entry scan is successful,<br>**When** committed,<br>**Then** stamp `EntryTime` with current time. |
